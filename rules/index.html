Classroom rules

The rules

  1. 01No food or drink.
  2. 02No calling out. Raise your hand.
  3. 03Stay in your seat unless I say you can get up.
  4. 04No feet on the furniture.
  5. 05Be in your seat when the bell rings.
  6. 06Phones go in the caddy.

Break rules 1 to 5 and you get an after-school detention. Your family will be told.

Phones follow district rules: a warning the first time, a detention the second, then the office takes over.
